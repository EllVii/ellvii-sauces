Let's make it saucy

Ask about the next tasting

Interested in joining a future pop-up, taking on the heat, or hosting Ell Vii's Sauces at your venue? Send us a request. We'll reply with available details.

Hot challenge safety: The extreme-heat option is for adults 18+ only, is entirely voluntary, and may not be available at every event. Ask about ingredients and allergens first. Skip hot peppers if they could pose a health risk for you. Participants can stop at any time.

Family-friendly tasting arrangements may vary by venue. A parent or guardian must supervise any minors. Media appearances involving minors require separate guardian authorization.
